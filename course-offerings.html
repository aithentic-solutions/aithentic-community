AI FOR NON TECHNICALS
Expected Audience Homemakers, lawyers, managers, students (arts)
Instructor TBD

This course is designed for nontechnical learners who want to understand and use Artificial Intelligence (AI) in their everyday lives — at work, school, or home. You don't need any background in coding or computer science; instead, the course focuses on how AI tools think, respond, and assist humans in solving daily problems. Through simple explanations, engaging examples, and hands-on activities, you'll explore how to use chatbots, image generators, voice assistants, and other AI-powered tools effectively. By the end of this course, you'll not only understand what AI is and how it works but also learn how to ask the right questions, think critically about AI outputs, and apply AI smartly to improve your productivity, creativity, and decision-making.
